# Tournament protocol

This is a reproducibility and review checklist for the Figment simulated Figgie-style market, not financial trading advice.

Rotate seating and opening hands across agent variants. Report number of rounds, seeds, and matchup counts. Keep ranking uncertainty visible: Elo differences based on a handful of games may not be meaningful. Save raw results for later recomputation.

## Evidence to retain
- Exact command and source commit
- Random seeds and scenario settings
- Raw outcomes and pass/fail assertions
- Known limitations and follow-up experiments
