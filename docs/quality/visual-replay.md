# Visual replay

This is a reproducibility and review checklist for the Figment simulated Figgie-style market, not financial trading advice.

When reviewing a round, align price movements with posterior updates, trades, and inventory changes. Verify chart labels reflect the same clock steps as underlying events. A visually plausible replay is not by itself an invariant or profitability test.

## Evidence to retain
- Exact command and source commit
- Random seeds and scenario settings
- Raw outcomes and pass/fail assertions
- Known limitations and follow-up experiments
