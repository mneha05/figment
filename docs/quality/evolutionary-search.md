# Evolutionary search

This is a reproducibility and review checklist for the Figment simulated Figgie-style market, not financial trading advice.

Keep the optimizer search budget and stopping conditions fixed across candidates. Log best and median fitness, diversity, and evaluation seed sets. Evaluate the selected policy on new seeds rather than quoting the best training fitness.

## Evidence to retain
- Exact command and source commit
- Random seeds and scenario settings
- Raw outcomes and pass/fail assertions
- Known limitations and follow-up experiments
