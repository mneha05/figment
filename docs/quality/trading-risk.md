# Trading risk

This is a reproducibility and review checklist for the Figment simulated Figgie-style market, not financial trading advice.

Track worst drawdown, inventory by suit, and late-game liquidation exposure along with total P&L. A higher mean return does not justify a strategy that frequently exceeds inventory or cash constraints. Distinguish realized profit from marked-to-model estimates.

## Evidence to retain
- Exact command and source commit
- Random seeds and scenario settings
- Raw outcomes and pass/fail assertions
- Known limitations and follow-up experiments
