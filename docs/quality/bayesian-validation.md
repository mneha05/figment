# Bayesian validation

This is a reproducibility and review checklist for the Figment simulated Figgie-style market, not financial trading advice.

Enumerate all 12 valid deck size assignments and check that posterior mass sums to one. Verify no hypothesis receives weight if it cannot contain the observed hand. Compare inferred goal-suit probabilities against hand-constructed small examples.

## Evidence to retain
- Exact command and source commit
- Random seeds and scenario settings
- Raw outcomes and pass/fail assertions
- Known limitations and follow-up experiments
